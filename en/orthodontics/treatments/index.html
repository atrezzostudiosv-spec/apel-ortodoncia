Types of Treatment

Apel Ortodoncia

Dental whitening or dental clearance

It's a dental treatment that allows to remove certain dental spots and that can be performed to a single tooth that's been darkened by some blow. It can also be performed to all teeth in general to clear the smile. Thus changing the natural color of the teeth or that acquired with the passing of time, thus creating a more "young" and pleasant smile to his face.

Piercing Fairy

That's a fashionable treatment in Europe, where a diamond-like tipstick or piercing is put. That piercing goes onto his tooth without his tooth suffering damage. That's reversible and has no consequences for mouth tissues.

Apnea and snoring treatment

When people do not have a very conciliatory dream, it affects their partners and those who have this disease as they are constantly tiring and in some serious cases can cause an even heart attack among other conditions.

Mouth protectors for sports

Sport are an essential part of human development but there is also a risk that accidents will occur by performing them, and the use of a mouth protector will greatly help as it greatly reduces the risk of damage to the teeth. Our protectors are individualised with more security and comfort than commercial ones. They are recommended to be used with contact sports or Olympic gymnastics. For more information, it will be a pleasure to help you. Just with your teeth, you can make sure your medal's gold.
