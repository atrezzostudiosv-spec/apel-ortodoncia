Interdisciplinary care

Since our treatments are comprehensive, we work closely with other colleagues as:

General dentists

Dental Surgeons (surgeries and complicated extractions)

Implantologists (specialists in replacing missing teeth)

Periodicists (specialists with gum and bone problems)

Medical care (child care)

Oral and endodoncists (channel treatment)

Loopedas (language therapists)

Pediatrics

If some of them need an interconsultation with us, we are always available, working with other colleagues we guarantee the best result for your health.
