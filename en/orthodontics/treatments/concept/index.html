Concept of treatment

Consultation: At first date we collect necessary medical information to start any dental treatment, conduct a clinical analysis of your teeth and your bite to see if an orthodontics treatment is necessary, if so we explain what your problems are and listen to what your treatment expectations are. To achieve this we have to have an appropriate diagnosis and we have to do this right here in the comfort of our clinic. Following a digital analysis of x-rays, study models and photographs an individualised treatment plan can be drawn up. That plan was drawn up by a specialist with years of experience. Because our care relies on our patients' satisfaction we are constantly updated and we have mastered different orthodontics and techniques that can guarantee their needs and expectations. From removable to fixed, from economic to discreet, from comfortable and ergonomic to fast, from visible to nearly invisible... and after an appropriate consultation to get to know your expectations we can advise you what's best treatment for you.... what you need we have.
