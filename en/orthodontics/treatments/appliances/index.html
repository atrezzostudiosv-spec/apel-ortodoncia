Type of apparatus and bracketing

Apel Ortodoncia

Retirement apparatology

It's especially used in children when there's a need to make some simple dental movements, these devices are very dear because they can be worked out in a great diversity of colors and they can be put images by making them unique and custom.

Functional apatology

That type of paratology is used to guide bone growth, these apparatus are quite comfortable and interferes with little or nothing with their tongue and get used to them quite quickly.

Raw and tidal apparatus

bad habits can deform teeth and influence with poor bone growth and development, we have appropriate treatment that will help with proper dental evolution.

Conventional Brackics

That type of paratology was used to make more complex dental movements and was greatly appreciated by children and young people as a different modulum color could be selected every once and for some time, making them light and modern.

Self-Reliable Brackics (Minor Filling)

That type of apatology is used if you wish to shorten treatment time as they offer less friction.

Aesthetic and autoligable bracketers

That type of bracketing combines the benefits of faster treatment and camouflage and bracketing will pass nearly unnoticed.

Aesthetic brackics

That type of bracketing is well appreciated when the aesthetics are primordial as their color similar to that of their teeth makes them get to pass almost unnoticed.

Lingual brackics

That type of paratology is used when the patient doesn't care to have the bracketing seen. The type of paratology used by our clinic offers some very comfortable bracketing.

Férulas (Invisalign and Clear-aligner)

Two methods with one can be more complex and with the other, both offer an insurmountable comfort and aesthetics.
