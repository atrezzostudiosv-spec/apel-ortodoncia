What is Invisible Orthodontics - Invisalign ®?

Invisalign ® solution to a range of Orthodontic problems (coatings, separate teeth, over-biting, cross-bites and more). Invisible tights (splints) are used as they are changed every two weeks. These lightens gradually move their teeth to their final position. You'll have a perfect smile in up to 18 months.

The Invisalign types with each case are:

Invisalign ® Full The Invisalign's basic treatment option to straighten your teeth with a number of clear and removable tights or splits.

Invisalign ® i7 for small dental movements. They are very short treatments that can last only 3 months.

Invisalign ® Teen includes all Invisalign ® Full functions with some modifications adapted to younger patients, such as performance indicators.

Vivalra ® Retainers To maintain the stability of the cases.

Invisalign ® Lite includes all Invisalign ® Full's functions with some modifications adapted to younger patients, such as performance indicators.
