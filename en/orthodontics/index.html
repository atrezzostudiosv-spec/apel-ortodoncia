Welcome to your Clinic Apel Orthodoncia

Why choose us?

Because we are a clinic that we offer more and more, we offer personalized care and we adapt ourselves to your needs. At our clinic you're first! You will find a pleasant and personal atmosphere constantly in training so that your experience will be insurmountable. Our materials are always high quality and doctors are highly professional specialists in their areas.

Second opinion

Today it's important to make sure that the treatment that you are about to get or are currently receiving is the right one...... that's why if you aren't seeing results in your treatment we can guide and give you a second opinion, so you can have a better picture of your situation.

Finance

For our treatments we offer different forms of payment and financing, with which you can be sure to cover your treatment payment. We guarantee that the cost of treatment will NOT be an excuse to avoid redressing your smile. We work with all credit cards and with all health insurance policies.

Location

We can easily be located as we are in a well-known and secure commercial area and you will have no problems with us. You can get to the clinic from different ways and we have sufficient parking space in front of the clinic for more comfort.

Security

You can get to your clinic and feel quiet as it's a secure area that's also guarded by security personnel.
