Help with loose bracketing

If a bracket is tipped off stay calm, if it doesn't cause any discomfort you can wait for your date to be rectified. If the bracket gets released from the wire we recommend saving it to be put later. Avoid eating bad food so that this doesn't happen again.

Loose wire, or pounding his cheeks or slipping out

If you get to drop the wire, we recommend trying to put it back into your position, this can be tried with a clamp, if this isn't achieved and cause some discomfort, we recommend using protective wax for Orthodontics, as a last option you can try to cut the wire if it's damaged your tissues. Make an appointment as soon as you can to help him.

A color module or an elastic chain have been dropped

The wire that goes through your bracketing will usually stay in place so you don't have to get alarmed, this can be put up at the next monitoring date. Don't let a few months go by if you attend your controls as this may delay your treatment.

Fixed Retener with a Loose Point

If you get to drop a point from your retainer we recommend that you make an appointment as soon as possible so that the retainer will be re-attached to your teeth. If this isn't quickly repaired, there's a risk that the tooth that remains unattached will move to its old position and a new retainer may be necessary. Asked an emergency date at the clinic.

Pain after your monitoring date

After his monitoring date, you are likely to get some discomfort with his teeth while chewing especially with hard foods, this sensation can be reduced by using soft foods and if necessary you can also resort to some tablet for dental pain.

Problems with your removable device

If your removable device isn't well adapted, cause excessive pain or pressure or some element of the device's making you uncomfortable, then you have to make an appointment so that you can solve your problem, as well as if your device gets lost.

as advice

To avoid taking off or damaging your regular equipment we recommend that you clear up hard foods and fruit with a firm consistency, so that you will be maintaining the good functioning of your treatment. You're not limited to soft foods, you just have to learn how to chew them. Maintaining proper hygiene of your teeth will avoid many problems that can even change the treatment time. If something gets to you or you have some doubt, you can always count on our help.
