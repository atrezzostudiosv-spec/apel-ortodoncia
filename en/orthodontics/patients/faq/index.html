Because right teeth are important? (Medical talking)

A proper dental position and a proper bite avoid problems with his skull and his chewing system, which can cause premature loss of teeth, problems with gum and bone, increases the incidence of caries, muscle and head pain and even his spine among other things. The pronunciation of words will be appropriate if your teeth are aligned and the chewing process improves by also helping with food digestion. It reduces the strain with temporomandibular union (mandible union with skull), if the bite isn't suitable, a sound called a snap and this leads to other more serious problems is generated in the joint. A treatment of orthodontics protects his teeth up to old age and improves his quality of life.

How long does an orthodontics treatment last?

The treatment time is directly related to the severity and bad position of your teeth and bones on average a treatment can last between 6 months and 3 years. A treatment of orthodontics should have forces that are biologically tolerable by the teeth hold system so as to avoid irreversible damage to their tissues. The teeth have to be stabilized after treatment has been completed so that the result of treatment will be sustained. Otherwise this can lead to a relapse that means that the teeth will have to come back to their old place.

To have an orthodontics treatment there are no age restrictions, if you are very young, the Orthodnist will tell you what age is ideal to start with your treatment, that depends on how bad you have and so will be the type of device that you are told.
