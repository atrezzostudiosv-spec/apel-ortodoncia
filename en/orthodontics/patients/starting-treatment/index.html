Treatment initiation

Consultation: At first date we collect necessary medical information to start any dental treatment, conduct a clinical analysis of your teeth and your bite to see if an orthodontics treatment is necessary, if so we explain what your problems are and listen to what your treatment expectations are.

Diagnosis: It continues with the collection of records for the proper development of an individualised treatment plan, models of plaster and x-rays (skull and panoramic) are taken and a photo-documentation is made, all these tools help us to achieve a successful treatment plan.

Plan delivery: The treatment plan, duration and costs of treatment are explained. Here we clear up every doubt that the patient may have.

Treatment is initiated and performed as planned, these treatments are individualised.

At the end of treatment, the placement of retainers that help to maintain their teeth is recommended as the right place until they are stabilized.

Here are some of the treatments that Dr. André Apel (Orthodontics and Orthodontics Maxilofacial specialist, graduate from Rostock University, Germany) works with his team of doctors and assistants at Apel Orthodontics.
