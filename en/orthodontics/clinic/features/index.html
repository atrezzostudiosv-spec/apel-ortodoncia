Clinical characteristics

Apel Ortodoncia

A wheelchair entrance for people.

3 Stretch areas with different entertainment options so that your waiting time will be pleasant.

Safe and accessible location with all transport.

Sterilization and disinfection, this is one of the most important areas and we have a strict and regular monitoring and sterilization of tools and work areas, thus guaranteeing protection to your health and to that of our personnel at all times.

Wide and secure parking with the clinic.

Rapid emergency dating.

Reparation of apparatus at short times as we have our own orthodontics lab where the technician under the advice of the Dr. will develop the necessary equipment for your treatment thus ensuring quality, efficiency and speed. We have a diversity of colors and images so that your devices are unique.

Treatment with considerations in allergic patients.

Wifi (internet).

Entertainment for children, youth and adults.

X-ray doesn't have to get you anywhere else to get your x-rays as we have the necessary equipment here.

Tooth brushing area: we have an exclusive area so that you can brush your teeth at any time, and we have everything we have to get your teeth and gums clear.
