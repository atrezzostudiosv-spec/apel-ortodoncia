Dental Cleaner

It's true that every daily routine and our obligations can lead us to delay visits as primordial and important as an annual dental cleanup. A date with the dentist, or hygienist, quickly and painless is necessary for our oral health and at the same time to improve dental aesthetics and to have a good smile. At Clínica Apel we put at your disposal all means necessary to conduct a best price oral cleaning.

What's dental cleanliness about?

Due to the benefits of a professional mouth cleanup, the enameling of the dental parts is strengthened and protected and the plate that forms with the gums and the spots caused by tobacco, coffee or wine and the slate under the gums or teeth are removed. The latter is a hard tank that accumulates into the teeth when the bacterial plate, made up of food remains and bacteria, isn't eliminated in time and mineralizes and became a frying pot. That substance adheres to the enamel of the teeth so strongly that it can only be eliminated with a professional cleanup. Sarre promotes the proliferation of bacteria that attack teeth and gums and if it isn't eliminated can cause gingivitis (inflammation of the gums), halitosis (bad breath) or periodontitis, that affects tissues that hold the teeth and, if not treated effectively, can end up with their loss. In the absence of a professional dental cleanup on time, the frying or dental calculation usually appears as a calcification of the bacterial plate by calcium salt reservoir. These salts come from saliva, A proper oral hygiene, and they cover not only the number of times we brush up a day but also the use and application of a proper dental brushing technique. Because a 100% hygiene is very difficult to achieve by our patients, dental prophylaxis, performed by professionals, is the only way to remove the already formed frying. The dental calculation will lead to irritation of the gum, leading first to a gingivitis that can trigger in a periodontitis and to subsequent loss of teeth in more serious cases. That's why it's important to have a oral cleanup to improve your oral health conditions. And with a good smile at Clínica Apel we have highly trained professionals who will offer you a high quality treatment, at an economic price with a friendly treatment and with a pleasant atmosphere. Also if you are going to have a dental whitening this type of treatment is fully recommended as it will help to make the result better and more effective.
