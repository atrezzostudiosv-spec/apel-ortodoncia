What are dental implants?

The dental implants are artificial roots that are put into your maxillary or mandibular bone to replace a lost tooth or group of teeth. They are made from biocompatible materials that have no rejection reaction and allow them to be bound to the bone.

How does the dental implant hold onto the bone?

The implant is placed into the patient's bone and after its placement the process of osteointegration occurs naturally. Osteointegration is a direct structural and functional connection between the living bone, ordered, and the surface of an implant undergoing functional load (Branemark, 1951). The process of osteointegration ranges from 3 to 6 months.

What's the use of dental implants?

The dental implants are used to replace teeth or group of teeth that have been lost by caries, infection, accident, trauma or periodontal disease. They achieve a functional and esthetic rehabilitation of the affected mouth or portion of the mouth.

FAQ about dental implants

What does dental implant treatment consist of?

The treatment consists of 3 or 4 phases or steps. The first is bone regeneration surgery if the place that will get the implant has insufficient bone. The second step would be surgery to place the implant or implants. Following this step, we have to hope that the time for an Osteointegration will pass. Once passed, the patient has been subjected to a small intervention called second surgery, with a minor incision into the gum and an implant entrance and a screw that will adapt the gum to receive the crown (artificial tooth). Between seven and fifteen days later, prints are taken for the production of the crown, bridge or prosthetic, and placed a week after the measures have been taken....

Initial phase of treatment study and planning

A panoramic x-ray or orthopantomography as well as a TAC to check the status, quality and quantity of bone available are necessary to plan the case. At Clínica Apel we have the technology to conduct both tests at our installations....

What care should I have after placement during the period of osteointegration?

During the period of osteointegration implants are covered with gums, so the only care will be to maintain good oral hygiene....

What's the maintenance phase?

The maintenance consists of applying to his dentist's appointments to conduct his monitoring and to keep his implants and prostheses at best. At Clínica Apel you will be called a few days before every review to remind you of your date with us....

How long will the treatment be sustained? Treatment with implants

The implants can last for many years as long as they care and the patient goes to his or her appointments and checks to give him or her proper maintenance....

What if an implant gets lost?

If you lose an implant, something that can happen, it will be replaced at no cost if lost during the warranty time. Any problem with the prosthetic will also be solved at no cost if within that period of guarantee of the prosthetic. To replace the lost implant, we have to wait about 3 or 4 months for the bone from the receiving area to heal and have once again the bone sufficient to house the implant....
