Infant odontology

The child's dentistry (odontopediatrics) is dedicated to the prevention and treatment of child's oral and dental illnesses. It's very important to have special attention to teeth and maxillary from the first few years of life

Preventive odontology and monitoring of dental rash.

Fissures sewer.

Obturations (pits).

Chopotomies.

Fluorization.

Extractions.

Orthodontics and monitoring of habits.

Oral health and proper development of the mouth are an integral part of the child's general health. The oral cavity should be studied from birth to detect potential anomalies. If there are no changes, a good time will be when his teeth start to come out (6 months - 1 year). The dentist should be used to care for children and especially have an integral vision of them. When milk teeth start to appear regular visits to the paediatrician and dentist are best and during replacement of the teeth are vital. Such visits should be sustained throughout childhood and adolescence.

Frequently asked questions

Is breastfeeding advisable?

Breast-feeding is of great importance for the child's physical, immune and affective development and also promotes a proper development of his or her mouth. It's important to have good oral hygiene after every drink, especially after the last drink at night....

How old can the pacifier be?

The use or non-use of the pacifier in newborns is a highly controversial issue. It would be great if they weren't necessary, but we all know that with the baby's persistent cry, the sucking can be an help. The ideal thing is to always use anatomical pacifiers with as little size as possible. Their use should not be prolonged beyond two and a half years of age, trying, if possible, to withdraw them earlier, as their persistence in time can lead to changes in the development of dental pits and cause malocclusions....

Is the use of bottles convenient?

Ideally, the child does not use the bottle while it can be breastfed, as apart from immunological, nutritional, psychological and mother-related benefits, breast-feeding allows for the proper development of the maids, promotes the proper location of the tongue in the oral cavity and thus the proper swallowing. At times we believe that a child does not get his or her breasts or takes enough milk and that his or her breast-feeding tends to be replaced with a bottle. Before making this important decision we have to consult with the paediatrician. If for some reason the bottle was necessary, a bottle with an anatomical, small orific tin should be used and require milk to be extracted and breathing through the nose. It should be obtained at pharmacies. In these cases, their use should not be prolonged beyond the year of age, as abusing them may cause problems with chewing, immediate and future deglutory and respiratory functions as well as alterations such as tooth malposition, eye palates or open biting.

My boy sucks his finger. What can I do?

Fingering is a common habit with children. That habit had been found even in his mother's womb. It should however be eliminated as soon as possible as it is capable of inducing changes in skeletal and dental development. To solve this harmful habit, the first option is to always convince the child to leave voluntarily. At the pharmacy there are some products, and they are not always effective, that paint their fingers and have bad taste. The child expert should be contacted to try a pact between children, parents and dentists. Some form of reward can also be sought to achieve the abandonment of digital suction. That reward should not be extravagant but especially motivating for the child. If the habit persist, with a work of conviction, you could choose to place devices that make digital suction difficult....

At how old do first teeth come up with milk?

What to do if his or her child's in pain because his or her teeth are coming out?

The rash of temporary dentition is often annoying and even painful and an irritability, increased salivation, decreased appetite and sometimes light febrica may occur. During this period, the child should be well hydrated and water intake should be increased, food temperature reduced and pipes and bottles chilled. The use of topical application products with sedative effects on gums is not recommended as it may interfere with the rash process. Paracetamol-type antithermal and analgesic, at appropriate doses, always under the supervision of the paediatrician, are an ideal measure. There are gum cheeseburgers on the market that are very useful to calm the pain from the rash. Your purchase should be made at pharmacies so that they are properly approved....

At how old do their teeth fall with milk?

The dental replacement, as with the rash, is also very variable. Each temporary tooth shall be receptacted with its relative permanent with the exception of temporary molars and replaced with presmolars. The fall of first temporary teeth and the appearance of final teeth coincide with 6 years old. It's important to note that it's at this time period (6 years) that the first final molar appears and erupts after the second "milk" tooth....

At how old do children have to brush their teeth?

The care, cleanliness or hygiene of the oral cavity should be performed from birth. The hygiene or cleaning of the gums should be performed after each intake of milk with sterile gauze. When first teeth erupt, they exist at a pharmacy with a high level of rubber and small brushes that parents can use to put their child into the habit of oral hygiene. Once the child is more autonomous, his or her daily hygiene can be performed with a small brush and with no toothpaste, under the supervision of his or her parents and once a day his or her parents have to do so. The dental paste can be used after 3 years and should be fluorinated (with a concentration of 1 100 ppm) and suitable brushes and pastes at these ages are available at pharmacies.

How many times a day do your teeth have to brush?

The brushing should be after every meal (breakfast, lunch and dinner). We have to note that it's very important that after the last brush, before sleep, food and drink should be consumed with the exception of water. That's why dental hygiene that takes place before going to bed is the most important....

On the other hand, if a temporary tooth is lost prematurely, the permanent may have no space to erupt, leading to malocclusions in final denture. What are sealants?

Sealants are a preventive method of caries in children. It consists of applying a resistant material to the motila motils and fissures. That material prevents caries from being put into them and their application is painless and thus performed without anaesthesia. They are a quick, efficient and easy method of prevention to apply.

My son's picking his teeth. What can I do?

The bad habit of fingering teeth, called bruxism, can occur both at day and at night and more often at night. It usually appears in children between the ages of 4 and 6. The triggering factors may be different and are often related to hyperactive children and stressful situations. If his habit remains, his teeth get serious wear and wear, leading to dentary pain and peri-oral musculature. Parents should consult with the child dentist for proper diagnosis and put into place appropriate therapeutic measures, ranging from treatment by a psychologist with anti-stress technologies to using protective accessories to prevent wear.
