What are Fairy Coronas?

The dental crowns are used to restore the function and aesthetics of structured teeth and dental implants. The metal-free ceramic crowns are made from different types of ceramics, with different indications. Depending on the case, best material is selected, seeking a balance between aesthetics and longevity. The dental crowns consist of fixed prostheses that are cemented to the natural tooth, and have been prepared earlier. We have improved the aesthetics of our teeth with our dental crowns as well as protecting them, especially with those teeth that are most weakened, such as endodonciated teeth.

What kind of dental corones do they exist?

Metal corones:

They're really tough and they're better at resisting wear. The only problem they have is that they're nothing aesthetic. Compare metal and porcelain crown.

Dairy crown of porcelain metal:

The inner part is metal while, at the outside, ceramics are used, thus giving them a more natural and similar look to the neighbouring teeth.

Porcelain dental crown:

That type of crowns are mainly used in the previous area, from canine to canine, given their great aesthetic contribution but are less resilient than those of metal-porcelain, because of their fragility they are used with caution. More information about porcelain pits (or ceramics)

Zirconium dental crown:

Zirconium's dental crowns are the most aesthetic and resilient, and for this reason they are best recommended by dentists. More information about zirconium cover (or Zirconio)
