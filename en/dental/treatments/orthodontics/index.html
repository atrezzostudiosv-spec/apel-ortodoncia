Orthodontics is a branch or specialty of dentistry intended to correct all those defects in the position of teeth that can have an aesthetic and functional impact on their occlusion. Through an extensive diagnosis, we can determine if an orthodontics treatment is required, our specialists will conduct a complete analysis of dentophacic characteristics and after the type of treatment has been decided they will be initiated.

Treatment of dental orthodontics

Orthodontic treatment may be indicated for people of any age, for children once they have been undergoing dental replacement and for adults of any age, but it's important to note that treatments in young people are usually faster as the movement of dental parts can be more easily performed while in adults after 50 years of age it may be a little longer, depending on the complexity of the case, the duration of treatment can be determined. A specialist orthodontics treatment brings a great deal of benefits as it improves the aesthetic appearance of teeth, facial expression, giving an appearance of rejuvenation, facilitates oral hygiene as by aligning dental parts it's easier to get rid of the food remains that accumulate in the interdental area, thus favouring the duration of teeth and improving the appearance of gums, improving the patient's self-esteem and physical and mental well-being.

Types of Orthodontics:

The difference between these orthodontics techniques means that they are placed with the dental part.

Conventional Orthodontics. (metal bracketing)

Invisible or esthetic orthodontics (sapphire or porcelain bracketing)

Lingual orthodontics.

Conventional orthodontics

Conventional orthodontics are those with traditional metal bracketers and they are placed on the vestibular face of teeth (front face) using arches with different diameters depending on the evolution of treatment. Through their placement and stay for 12 to 24 months at the patient's mouth, bad placement of his or her teeth and meeting the targets set by his or her treatment are achieved.

What's the need for orthodontics?

Mainly to make our dental parts suffer less and avoid other dental problems in the future, beyond mere aesthetic issues. The aims of proper dental orthodontics treatment should be:

Treatment should have as its main task to improve the patient's oral health.

Having a well-placed teeth, it's far easier to get cleaned up with what we prevent problems with caries and gums.

Recent studies have shown that an incorrect bite or occlusion of the teeth can cause muscle discomfort and cause pain in the head, neck, shoulder or back.

We also improve the aesthetics of our teeth and harmony with our face, that is, a more beautiful smile.
