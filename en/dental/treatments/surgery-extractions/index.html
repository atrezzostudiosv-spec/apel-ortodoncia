Oral surgery

At Apel Clinic, tooth extraction is a routine procedure. There's nothing to be afraid of, and our team of experts will guide you throughout the process. Under special circumstances, oral surgery is necessary to treat pain and damage to your teeth. But don't let his name scare him. Unlike traditional surgery, oral surgery rarely requires hospitalisation. In fact, many oral surgery procedures that occur at our clinics, including teeth extracted, teeth extracted from judgment, dental implants and bone grafts, simply require local anesthesia and some over-the-counter analgesics.

Teeth extraction

The adult teeth last with time in most cases but sometimes, a tooth should be removed. The trauma, serious cavities, infections and stacking are typical reasons for a tooth's extraction. The extraction of judgment teeth is also a common reason for extraction, especially between the ages of 17 and 25 (that's when these later great molars usually appear). At Apel Clinic, tooth extraction is a routine procedure. There's nothing to be afraid of, and our team of experts will guide you throughout the process.

Extraction of judgment teeth

One of the most common reasons for an extraction is the extraction of judgment teeth (especially for adolescents and young adults). Since they are the last permanent teeth to come out, it's possible that the teeth of judgment do not have the space they need and tend to push the other teeth, leading to damage to their teeth and pain. The dental surgeons at Clínica Apel can use x-rays to determine if you or your teenage child need to have an extraction of the teeth from the judgment before they cause some damage or pain.

Fatal implants

The dental implants are small bolts similar to screws that suit the jaw bone and are used to secure an artificial tooth. They are usually made from light titanium or titanium alloys and are designed to last a lifetime. In reality, dental implants merge into the bone, making them exceptionally strong and durable. Dental implants can be an excellent choice to replace a missing tooth. The best of all is that the dental implant procedure can be performed at Apel Clinic. In some cases, the tooth implant can be placed on the same day as the tooth's extraction.

Fairy bone grafts

The loss of a tooth does not only affect its physical appearance but also damage the bone that serves as a support and surrounds the missing tooth. In fact, this bone that provides support can deteriorate and shrink with time. Apel Clinic recommends bone grafts to avoid loss of the support bone. Although it may seem scary, bone grafts are unusual and painless procedures that involve the filled up of cages in the jaw with bone material processed in a lab. Over time, the body fills the area with new bone and soft tissue, helping to adjust the tooth to the jaw.
