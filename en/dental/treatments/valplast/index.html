Valpast ®

Valpast ®, provides us with unique physical and aesthetic properties and with a perfectly proven functionality and so it's ideal as long as we are considering removable as partial, unilateral and complete prostheses, both superior and lower. Two of the most important points we get at using this material are, functionality, that care about the odontologist, and aesthetics, what the patient really wants. Adherence, providing more comfort and stability in the mouth, its two-mm dregadez, competes against the four or five conventional acrylic, does not alter with fluids, being stable with the passage of time, as, as it does not break, or fissures, hypoallergenic and nontoxic, allows us to use them for all patients in known cases. That added to its irbreakability and flexibility, means that Valplast ® meets the requirements required by the demanding Dodontologist, as its low visibility, light and transparency make the patient gain an aesthetic prosthetic with which they have no credit to smile and appear in public, while also providing them with great confidence while eating. Valplast ®, uses Retento-Grip ® Tissu-Bearing (retensive hook and tissue rest). Due to this technique, the painful work of preparing natural teeth or tissues isn't necessary. The authorized Processor Laboratory requires only a model of an alginate printing, an antagonist model and its respective bite. Printing boxes are unnecessary and about biting and mouth testing can be taken for more precision in Kennedy's 1 classification cases. We therefore consider that Valpast ® should be used to patients with an allergy to poly methacrylate, pediatric (space maintenance) prostheses, patients with periodontal disease, occlusal femulas, palatines shutters, patients with palatines or mandibular torus, as well as any type of bone exostosis in which his or her pre-Protestant and generic surgical treatment is inappropriate, as long as his or her removeable prostheses are indicated. Valplast ®, as can be assumed, remains an additional solution for those who have been missing all their dental parts and we have to highlight the different disadvantages they have with using this material, such as difficulties with retouching at a clinic as we have no custom of manipulating this product and the need for machinery and specific materials for production at the Laboratory. In addition to the contraindications of patients with advanced palatine intolerance with high resilent alveolar reduds and with an established prosthetic examination.
