Endodoncia

We have specialists in endodontics, one of the most difficult to find within dentistry. An endodontics should be used when the pulp, a small strand-shaped fabric found at the centre of the tooth, is damaged. That usually occurs as a result of an infection. When it happens, this pulp should be extracted and it is necessary to clear and complete the space that remains inside the tooth and the root ducts. Using an endodontics we get the duct sealed without have to resort to the extraction and replacement of the piece, something that did have been about years ago. That way, and because of the presence of endodoncysts at all our clinics, we have been able to save dental parts that were previously lost. That's why your smile will continue to be radiant after undergoing endodontics. The endodontics is a treatment with a vital role to play with gutapercha, a natural material obtained from the sap of some trees. The gutapercha can be used to purify the root ducts of the tooth, an essential thing for endodontics to be a success. On our blog you can find a number of questions and answers about treatment of root duct or endodontics.

Why do I have to get an endodontics?

The infection of the tooth pulp is a very difficult disease to detect, even at specialist's consultations. It can be caused by other problems such as tooth damage, small breakages, a profound cavities or even performing other invasive dental treatments that have been likely to have affected some of the teeth. That's why and once again from Clínica Apel we recommend to go to the dentist with any suspicion, malaise or symptoms of an oral and dental disease. A time visit can be essential to help you achieve optimal dental health and provides an advantage that gets lost if you don't come regularly to check your mouth and the problem gets serious.
