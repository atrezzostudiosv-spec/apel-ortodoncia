Dental whitening

Get a good and bad smile with the dental whitening treatments our specialist dentists will be able to conduct at our clinics. Do you want to have a more white tone in your teeth? At Clinica Apel we have trained professionals to guide you about any dental treatment and conduct it, in this case about dental whitening. Many people are unaware of the meaning of dental whitening, we can refer to this as an aesthetic treatment that is performed to clear up the original tone of the dental parts, giving them a more white look and achieve a harmony in the color of the teeth. At Clínica Apel we are capable of offering a competitive dental whitening treatment with complete safety and a perfect result.

What's with the dental whitening treatment?

A gel that contains hydrogen peroxide (varies its percentage) is applied to the surface of the teeth to be clarified and the light with led light, that activates the oxygen release process, enters the enamel and produces clearance effect on the tooth, can reduce up to 4 tones of target. It is important to know that good oral hygiene should be available to conduct this treatment with good results and should preferably be performed after a recent dental cleansing and at the same time the patient should be aware that this is an abrasive treatment with led light, that if done often it would weaken the enamel of the teeth leading to sensitivities, and that the recommended time between them is about 3 years. The following are recommendations after dental whitening treatment, and that allow to look at the perfect smile:

Avoid consumption of red wine, tea or other beverages with artificial dyes.

Maintain good and common oral hygiene.

Do a proper brushing technique every day.

Avoid any kind of food with a lot of dyes.

The first 72 hours after treatment are completely prohibited.
